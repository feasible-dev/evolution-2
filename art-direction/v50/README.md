# v50 unified press gallery

One generated environment replaces the separate opponent and analyst backdrops.
The painted desk, gallery beam and LED recess form one physical structure.
Speech bubbles fit their text and identify the speaker through their pointer.
The standing atlas has additional headroom; seated sprites retain their native size.
Hand props and future desk accessories are intentionally deferred.

The generated contact sheet was normalized to a 6-by-3 atlas of 192px square
tiles. The completed standing poses replace Gerard frames 2 through 13 only.
The original rest, entry and settle frames remain in use with 64px of transparent
padding above them. The eight bottom rows containing the old blue desk are omitted
at display time; character pixels stay intact. Both presenters use the taller display canvas. The top of
the game viewport provides the final crop; there is no lower portrait-frame crop.

Assets generated with the built-in image_gen tool. Prompts are in prompts.json.
